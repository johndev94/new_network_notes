Testing was carried out using **VigorAP 1062C and VigorAP 962C** on firmware **5.1.0.1 and 5.1.1**, with both a **Vigor 2866ax (DrayOS 4)** and **Vigor 2767ax (DrayOS 5)**, in **Mesh and non-Mesh configurations**. On firmware **5.1.1**, wireless clients connected to different SSIDs assigned to the **same LAN** are unable to communicate, while clients connected to the **same SSID** communicate normally. The issue was reproduced with both AP models, both DrayOS 4 and DrayOS 5 routers, and with and without Mesh.

After downgrading the VigorAP 962C from **5.1.1 to 5.1.0.1**, clients on different SSIDs assigned to the same LAN could communicate normally. This indicates the issue was introduced in **5.1.1**. Expected behaviour is for clients on different SSIDs assigned to the same LAN to communicate unless client isolation or a similar isolation feature is enabled. **Firmware 5.1.0.1 works as expected.**
