Make sure in the configuration of your AP's, under Wireless LAN (2.4GHz) >> General Setup: Isolate Member option is unticked for all AP's. The same applies for Wireless LAN (5GHz) >> General Setup. The same setting must be "disabled/ unticked" on the router.  
  
If they are on the same network range there is no special setup for your sonos kit. You connect it to the network as advised by the manufacturer. There is no specific guide on how to setup sonos with Draytek.  
  
  
  
If the sonos device is having difficulty connecting I recommend enabling IGMP Services. Navigate to Applications >> IGMP and enable both options, apply and reboot the router.
