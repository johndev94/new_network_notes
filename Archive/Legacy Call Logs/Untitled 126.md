2866ax 

copper to fiber


You won’t need an external modem with the new Vigor 2866ax. The router has a built-in modem for fibre over copper (FTTC/VDSL), so it can connect directly to your phone line. If you are on full fibre (FTTP), your provider will have given you an ONT (the small fibre box). The 2866ax can connect straight into that using its Ethernet WAN port.

So whether you’re on copper-to-fibre or full fibre, the 2866ax will handle the connection directly without the need for any extra modem.
