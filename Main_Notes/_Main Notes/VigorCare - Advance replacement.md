VigorCare 

 Core VigorCare Benefits 

- Enhanced warranty with 3 year or 5 year options. 

- Includes advanced replacement of faulty hardware. 

- Designed to minimise downtime for business-critical devices. 

- Covers DrayTek routers, switches and access points. 


Advanced Replacement 

- Support confirms the fault first. 

- Replacement unit is shipped same day for next working day delivery (most UK areas). 

- DrayTek covers the shipping costs. 

- We aim to deliver the new device and collect the faulty unit on the same day, but if installation time is needed you can return the old device later. 

- You keep the replacement unit, and the VigorCare transfers to the new device. 


Warranty and Coverage 

- Extends warranty, no parts or labour costs for eligible hardware faults. 

- Still follows normal troubleshooting process before replacement approval. 

- Firmware or configuration issues are handled through standard support. 


Eligibility and Activation 

- VigorCare must be purchased and registered within 6 months of product purchase. 

- Subscription is bought through a DrayTek dealer. 

- Different VCARE tiers exist depending on device class. 


Operational Notes 

- Delivery to remote areas or Ireland may take longer or include surcharge. 

- Main goal is faster hardware recovery and reduced downtime.
