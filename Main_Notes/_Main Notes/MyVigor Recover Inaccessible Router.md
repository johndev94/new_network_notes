Guidelines for Recovering a Router Registered to an Inaccessible MyVigor Account 

If a customer cannot access the MyVigor account linked to their router, please follow these steps to verify ownership and assist with recovery: 

Verification Requirements 

The customer must provide two of the following pieces of information (either 1 and 3, or 2 and 3): 

1. Proof of Purchase 
    

- A copy of the original purchase receipt or invoice for the device. 
    

2. Remote Access to the Router 
    

- Grant us remote access to the router so we can log in. 
    

- Confirm that you can successfully log into the router. 
    

- Verify the device model and LAN MAC address in the router’s interface match the device the customer wants to recover. 
    

3. Photograph of the Device 
    

- Provide a clear image of the back of the device showing both the serial number and the PIN code printed on it.
