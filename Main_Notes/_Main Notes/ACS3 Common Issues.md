
### Devices appearing as active
The issue with the devices appear as active even though they are offline. ACS only knows a device is offline if it's failed to connect to it.


### Devices show loading screen when configuring
With the device getting a loading screen during configuration, this is probably due to the device is not properly configured.


### Error: DrayDDNS license not found

Error: DrayDDNS license not found" in the logs. However, when he logs into the My Vigor it shows renewed.

Solution: Login to myvigor from the router's product registration page and force sync on Drayydns
