**Note:**  
Customer reported that both DrayTek V3910 and V3912 routers have had issues connecting to Cisco routers via SFP modules for fibre connections. The modules in use were 10Gtek Single Mode SFP duplex, Cisco-coded. In some cases, the link worked for up to a year before failing. The failure was resolved by replacing the fibre SFP connection with DrayTek DAC cables. The customer now plans to replace all fibre connections with DAC cables to avoid recurrence. Possible cause may be related to SFP module coding compatibility between devices.
