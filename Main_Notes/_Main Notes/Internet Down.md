Thank you for contacting DrayTek Technical Support.

As discussed on the call, your devices can connect to the Vigor 2135ax over Ethernet and WiFi, however they show no internet. This indicates that the issue is not with the router but with the internet service itself.

You will need to contact your landlord to get the ISP details and possibly the account information. You can then contact the ISP and confirm whether the service is currently down. If the line is down, there is nothing we can fix from our side and you will need to wait for the ISP to restore the service.

If the ISP confirms that the line is active, you will need to request the setup details for the router. Ask whether the service uses PPPoE or static or dynamic IP. If PPPoE, request the username and password. If static, request the IP address details.

Once we have the correct information, we can factory reset the 2135ax and enter the required settings to restore the internet connection.
