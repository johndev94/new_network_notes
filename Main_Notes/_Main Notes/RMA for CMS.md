Thank you for reaching out to us. 

  
To request a Return Merchandise Authorisation (RMA) number, please contact or customer services team, using our [contact form](https://store.cmsdistribution.com/contact "https://store.cmsdistribution.com/contact").

Alternatively, fill in the Returns Request Form [(Click here to Download form)](https://store.cmsdistribution.com/media/wysiwyg/Education-Centre/RMA_request_form_CMS_Store_PDF.pdf "https://store.cmsdistribution.com/media/wysiwyg/education-centre/rma_request_form_cms_store_pdf.pdf") and send it to our customer services team: [customer.services@cmsdistribution.com](mailto:customer.services@cmsdistribution.com "mailto:customer.services@cmsdistribution.com")

For further information, please view our [returns policy](https://www.cmsdistribution.com/terms-conditions#returns "https://www.cmsdistribution.com/terms-conditions#returns")

Please note: The RMA must be returned within 14 days from the date of issue of the RMA number.
