## Description
Customer reports that after upgrading the Vigor C410ax to firmware version 5.3.8, the SIM slot activity LED no longer illuminates, despite the SIM connection continuing to function correctly.

The issue was initially reported after the firmware upgrade from 5.3.6.1 to 5.3.8 and we have subsequently replicated. The cellular connection remains operational, however the front panel LED that indicates SIM activity does not light up as expected.

This appears to be a firmware-related issue affecting the LED status indication rather than the cellular functionality itself.

## Steps to reproduce
1. Upgrade a Vigor C410ax to firmware version 5.3.8 from 5.3.6.1.
2. Insert a valid SIM card and establish a cellular data connection.
3. Confirm that mobile internet connectivity is working correctly.
4. Observe the SIM slot activity LED on the front panel is not lit up.

## Notes

- Issue first reported by customer following upgrade to firmware 5.3.8.
- Support has successfully replicated the behavior.
- No impact observed on cellular connectivity or data transmission.
- Appears to be limited to the LED indication function.
- Downgrading back to 5.3.6.1 rectifies the issue.
