Customer reports that their internet was previously working using the static IP 172.21.236.73 with gateway 172.21.236.72 on WAN2, and an IP Alias of 62.64.202.228. The connection has since stopped working.  
  
ISP has confirmed that the 62.64.202.228 address should not be applied on the WAN interface. It should be removed as a WAN IP Alias and instead applied on the LAN interface. Because NAT is not being used in this setup, they recommend assigning a dummy VLAN1 address and then applying the 62.64.x.x address to VLAN2.  
  
ISP also stated that the device connected to the DrayTek must be configured with the following WAN details:  
  
WAN IP: 62.64.202.228  
Subnet Mask: 255.255.255.254  
Gateway: 62.64.202.229  
  
ISP can see the MAC of the DrayTek from their gateway, so from their side the line and routing look fine. They believe the issue is local and related to how the addressing has been applied.
